What's in a pack

One city, done properly

Each pack covers one city: the bridges, towers and stadiums you look for on approach.

Placement
Footprints, spans and heights come from OpenStreetMap survey data, so every structure stands where it does in real life.
Replaces autogen
Clean exclusion zones remove the default generic buildings underneath, so nothing doubles up or clips.
Frame rate
Each model ships with levels of detail, so the city stays smooth at low altitude.
Night lighting
Bridges and towers light up at dusk, for night flights and sunset approaches.
Both sims
One download installs in MSFS 2020 and MSFS 2024.
Support
Install help and fixes in the forum. Replies within 48 hours.
